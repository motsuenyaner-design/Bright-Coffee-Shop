OVERVIEW

The Coffee Shop dataset contains transactional sales data from multiple store locations. 
It includes information on products sold, sales transactions, dates, times, and store branches. 
The dataset is used to analyze business performance, identify top-selling products, understand customer purchasing patterns, track sales trends, and compare the performance of different stores.

This analysis helps the coffee shop make data-driven decisions to improve sales, optimize operations, and enhance customer satisfaction.

SUMMARY

The analysis of the Coffee Shop sales data revealed important business insights. 
Coffee and tea products were the main drivers of sales, contributing significantly to overall revenue. 
Sales were higher during peak hours, particularly in the morning and early afternoon, reflecting typical customer purchasing behavior. 
Store performance varied across locations, with some branches consistently generating more sales than others.
The findings also highlighted top-selling product categories and provided valuable information on sales trends, helping identify opportunities to improve inventory management, marketing strategies, and overall business performance.

PROCESS

Collected and explored the coffee shop sales dataset to understand its structure and key variables.
Performed data cleaning to remove inconsistencies and ensure data quality.
Used SQL queries in Databricks to examine transaction dates, operating hours, store locations, and product categories.
Analyzed sales patterns across products, stores, and time periods.
Identified key trends, top-performing products, peak sales periods, and store performance differences.
Interpreted the results and presented insights through visualizations and summaries to support business decision-making.
